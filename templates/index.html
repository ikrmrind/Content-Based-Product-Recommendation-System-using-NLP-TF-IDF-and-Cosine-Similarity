How the engine works

From raw text to recommendations in four steps

01

Clean

Product tags are lowercased, stripped of punctuation and stopwords, then lemmatized with NLTK.

02

Vectorize

TF-IDF with unigrams + bigrams turns every product into a 10,000-dimension vector.

03

Compare

Cosine similarity measures the angle between the chosen product and every other product.

04

Recommend

The top matches are returned with a similarity score and shown visually with their images.
